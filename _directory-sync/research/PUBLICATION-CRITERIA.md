# Publication criteria — Malaysia and Japan

These criteria decide which researched records are **recommended** for publication. Publication itself still
requires the owner's approval (`_directory-sync/publication.json`). Singapore is governed separately and is unchanged.

## Classes that may appear in the directory
| Class | Evidence required | Public label |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Halal-certified (verified) | The certifying body's own register or list names this outlet / premises, with a valid expiry on the date checked | "Halal-certified (certifier, confirmed DD MMM YYYY)" |
| 2. Muslim-friendly declaration | The establishment's own published statement, or a tourism body publishing information the establishment supplied | "Self-declared Muslim-friendly" or "Muslim-friendly — listed by [tourism body] (establishment-reported)" |
| 3. Selected halal / Muslim-friendly menu | As class 2, where only some dishes or a special menu are offered | adds "selected halal menu only" |
| 4. Muslim-owned | A source states the owner is Muslim (establishment-reported in a tourism guide, or the establishment itself) | adds "Muslim-owned (as reported)"; never implies certification |
| 5. Community-suggested | OpenStreetMap or similar public report only | "Community-suggested — not verified" — **not recommended at this stage** |

"Certification reported — not confirmed" (claimed by the establishment, an operator for its whole chain, a tourism body or the
press, but not found on the certifier's own register) is shown with that wording and is never shown as certified.

## A record is recommended only when all of these hold
- **C1 Identity and location** — a street address from an official or primary source (certifier register, tourism body,
  establishment or operator). For Malaysian chain outlets the JAKIM premises register is the official source.
- **C2 Evidence of practice** — from the certifier, the establishment itself, or a tourism body publishing information the
  establishment supplied. News, university or community guides alone are not enough.
- **C3 Currency** — latest evidence no older than 3 years at the date checked.
- **C4 No unresolved issue** — no reported closure, no conflicting source, not a vegetarian/vegan venue without a halal or
  Muslim-friendly claim, not a chain JAKIM has stated is uncertified, not an operator statement that it is not halal.
- **C5 Label discipline** — the public label is derived from the strongest supported evidence only.

## Malaysian chain outlets and the JAKIM register (checked 18 Oct 2026)
The JAKIM premises register (myehalal.halal.gov.my, Premis Makanan) was searched by chain name in a browser session;
only derived facts for our own outlets are kept (`jakim-outlets-my.csv`: register premises name, expiry, method, date).
An outlet is **confirmed** only when the same chain has exactly one register premises at the outlet's postcode and that
premises is not matched to any other mapped outlet. Name-only matches and premises shared by several mapped outlets are
**review** (not certified); outlets with no match stay "Certification reported — not confirmed" and are not recommended.
In Japan an establishment's claim about *itself* may be shown as "Certification reported — not confirmed"; in Malaysia a
chain operator's claim about all its outlets is not an outlet-level claim and is not recommended on its own.

## Not recommended (kept as research candidates)
Community-only OpenStreetMap records (including the Miri records), chain outlets not found on the certifier's register,
records with only third-party mentions, stale or conflicting evidence, and records without a street address.
